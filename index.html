about

I'm Arsh Sahay, a Data Engineer at Digihumans Technologies. For the last two years I've been turning messy, unstructured data into clean, LLM-ready knowledge, and building the cloud pipelines on AWS and Azure that move it around.

Most of my recent work is in retrieval. I architected a hierarchical RAG on FastAPI and Qdrant, built a hybrid dense + BM25 pipeline with Cohere reranking, and extended it with action-based capabilities like sending email and updating CRM records. That system powers a production Unreal Engine digital human.

I came to engineering from commerce: a B.Com (Hons) in Finance, then an MCA in Data Science from Jain University. Outside work, I self-host agentic side projects on a Raspberry Pi 5.
